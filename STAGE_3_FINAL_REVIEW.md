## 5. Hero Differentiation Review

- P01 reads as a single-vial system.
- P02 reads as a coordinated pair through its distinct two-container composition.
- P03 reads as five-unit organization.
- P04 is visibly more presentation-driven than P03 through graphite outer packaging, ten vials, and a 5 + 5 fitted insert.
- P05 reads as a premium dropper serum, with a visible frosted bottle and pipette.
- P06 reads as a discrete airless pump system and is clearly distinct from P05 because it contains no dropper or pipette.

Conclusion: Hero differentiation is sufficient for the concept stage. P03 is the weakest only because its same source image also carries later sections, not because its five-unit logic is unclear.

## 6. Exploded View Differentiation Review

- P01 explains the single-vial assembly sequence.
- P02 explains dual-component separation and pairing.
- P03 explains five-position organization, but reuses its primary concept image rather than a dedicated exploded composition.
- P04 clearly separates premium outer box, ten-cavity insert, ten vials, and closures.
- P05 separates carton, insert, bottle, collar, dropper, and pipette.
- P06 separates carton, insert, overcap, actuator, pump, bottle body, and simplified inner container.

All views have a distinct system purpose. P03 is the first future replacement priority because it has the least differentiated exploded image.

## 7. Component Differentiation Review

The component sections are not simply renamed copies:

- P01 focuses on vial closure layers and single-carton fit.
- P02 adds A/B primary containers, two closure directions, and the dual-cavity insert.
- P03 adds standardized repetition, neutral identification, and five-cavity organization.
- P04 adds high-count repetition, ten-cavity insert, premium outer box, and presentation inspection.
- P05 adds bottle, dropper, pipette, collar, decoration, and optional insert.
- P06 adds airless body, simplified inner dispensing structure, pump, actuator, collar, overcap, and optional insert.

The visual component-section pattern is intentionally shared by the frozen template. Only P03 needs a more distinct future component material/detail image.

## 8. Customization Differentiation Review

- P01-P04 appropriately focus on vial finish, closure, insert, carton, and quantity where relevant.
- P05 shifts to bottle finish, 15/30 mL direction, dropper, collar, and skincare decoration.
- P06 shifts to bottle finish, 15/30/50 mL direction, pump/actuator, overcap, and integrated carton options.

There is controlled overlap in color and finishing options, but the component categories make the commercial differences clear. No unnecessary catalog-like SKU density was found.

## 9. Premium Hierarchy

- P04 is the highest-premium vial kit. The graphite box, symmetric ten-vial arrangement, and bespoke ten-cavity insert create a clear step above P03.
- P05 is more refined and beauty-led through frosted glass, dropper proportion, and material presentation.
- P06 is more modern and integrated than P05 through the continuous airless body, actuator, overcap, and pump architecture.

The hierarchy reads as Core Biotech (P01/P02), Multi-Vial Kits (P03/P04), and Premium Skincare Dispensing (P05/P06).

## 10. Biotech vs Skincare Grouping

The visual grouping is perceptible without changing page structure:

- Core Biotech: P01 and P02.
- Multi-Vial Kits: P03 and P04.
- Premium Skincare Dispensing: P05 and P06.

A future Peptide Packaging Solution Landing Page should use these three groupings as its primary browse paths, rather than presenting six undifferentiated cards.

## 18. Current Known Issues

- P03 needs dedicated exploded and open-box photography/rendering to reduce reliance on one source image.
- P03 emits a non-blocking Next.js development-mode LCP advisory for its reused concept image. Keep the existing Hero priority behavior; reassess production LCP when the dedicated P03 assets are available.
- All current product visuals are AI concepts and should not be represented as final client or production photography.
- The shared template intentionally produces a consistent long-form rhythm. It is appropriate for the series, though mobile detail pages are long.
- The in-app browser session does not expose a readable console-log collection API. No visible hydration interruption, broken image, or runtime serving failure was observed; network waterfall was not inspected.

## 19. Formal Freeze Decision

P01-P06 can be formally frozen at the current concept stage.

- Product logic is differentiated.
- Hero, exploded, components, and customization data communicate distinct systems.
- SEO and related-system relationships are valid.
- Responsive samples have no overflow and all loaded images were available.
- `npm run lint` and `npm run build` pass; build generated all 33 routes.

The remaining work is approved asset replacement, final performance measurement against real photography, and optional visual polish based on stakeholder review. None of this blocks the current freeze.

## 20. Next Stage Recommendation

### Preferred: Option A - Stage 4A, Peptide Packaging Solution Landing Page

The site now has six complete peptide system detail pages and a frozen Homepage, but it lacks a dedicated peptide-level decision and discovery page. A solution landing page is the strongest next step because it can group Core Biotech, Multi-Vial Kits, and Premium Skincare Dispensing before a visitor chooses an individual system.

### Option B - Stage 4B, Skincare Packaging

Defer until Stage 4A is complete. P05/P06 give a strong visual base, but a skincare line should not be started before the peptide portfolio gains its dedicated discovery layer.

### Option C - Stage 4C, Components / Packaging Category System

Defer until after Stage 4A. Components are useful for technical browsing, but a peptide solution landing page has clearer commercial value and should route visitors into the completed P01-P06 portfolio first.

No Stage 4 work was started.
